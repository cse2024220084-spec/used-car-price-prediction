Why Predictions Differ from the Actual Market Price

1. Actual Market Price: Represents the raw median price recorded in historical transaction data for this specific Make, Model, and Manufactured Year returned directly by the backend API.

2. Random Forest Model Adjustment (Bagging): Random Forest constructs 100 parallel decision trees. It averages continuous inputs (e.g., exact mileage wear, horsepower, transmission) to prevent overfitting, resulting in a conservative and stable estimate.

3. XGBoost Model Adjustment (Gradient Boosting): XGBoost sequentially builds decision trees to minimize residual errors. It heavily penalizes wear factors (like high mileage) and rewards premium power options, capturing complex non-linear price drops.

4. Summary: The machine learning models adjust the static historical actual price up or down based on your vehicle's specific combination of mileage, horsepower, transmission, and fuel type.
