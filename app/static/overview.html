Used Car Price Prediction
(Machine Learning)

Welcome to our Machine Learning term project! This application leverages advanced regression models (Random Forest and XGBoost) to accurately predict the resale value of used cars based on historical market data. Our goal is to provide a highly accurate, interactive tool for both buyers and sellers to estimate fair market prices.
