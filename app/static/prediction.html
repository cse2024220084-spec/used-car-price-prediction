Why Predictions Differ from the Actual Market Price

1. Actual Market Price: The median of all matching records in used_car_sales.csv filtered by all 6 features (Manufacturer, Car Name, Car Type, Year, Fuel Type, Transmission).

2. Random Forest (Bagging): Averages 100 parallel decision trees trained on the same 6 features. Produces stable, conservative estimates that closely track the dataset median.

3. XGBoost (Gradient Boosting): Sequentially builds trees to minimize residual errors. Captures subtle non-linear patterns in the feature combinations, sometimes predicting slightly above or below the dataset median.

4. Summary: Both models are trained exclusively on the 6 features you select. Differences arise from how each algorithm weighs feature interactions and handles the distribution of prices within each feature combination.
